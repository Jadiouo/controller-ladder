# spike10b: whip with realistic conditions, H11 (learned vs classical identification) and H12 (residual RL)

All numbers are in `table.md` (generated by `experiments/spike10b/report10b.py` from the raw files). This README gives the protocol as actually run, the budget on three axes, the priors each side received, every deviation from hypothesis card v6/v7/v7.1, and the limitations. Statements marked **(inference)** are interpretation, not measurement.

## 1. Result in one paragraph

best-classical = **T-adapt3-full** (model-fit identification with per-link masses + viscous damping + Coulomb friction + length scale, 13 parameters; chosen on holdout 1100-1399, 69.7 %). On the primary endpoint TEST-B (seeds 20000-20499, n=500, one locked run): best-classical 65.6 %, L-est-full 66.5 % (3 training seeds), R-PPO-hist 65.6 % (1 training seed), T-oracle 89.2 %. **H11 NOT supported** (L-est-full minus best-classical = +0.9 pp, 95 % CI [-3.5, 5.4], Holm p = 1.0). **H12 NOT supported** (R-PPO-hist minus best-classical = 0.0 pp, CI [-4.0, 4.0], Holm p = 1.0). TEST (n=50) agrees in direction (L-est-full -2.7 pp, R-PPO-hist +2.0 pp, both CI containing 0). Headroom exists (oracle +23.6 pp over best-classical), but neither learned method recovers any of it.

## 1b. Secondary results (details and CIs in `table.md`)

* **L-est vs T-adapt3 (identification, TEST-B)**: L-est-full minus T-adapt3-full +0.9 pp [-3.5, 5.3]; minus T-adapt3-grad +15.5 pp [10.5, 20.5] (the low-dimensional gradient parametrisation is clearly worse than learned regression; the full per-link parametrisation is not). On holdout (n=300, selection set) L-est-full is 63.7 % vs 69.7 % (-6.0 pp [-12.1, 0.2]).
* **L-est-eff** (no per-link labels): TEST-B 64.4 % vs full 66.5 % (full minus eff = +2.1 pp, CI [-1.1, 5.2]); holdout 62.1 %. The per-link mass labels are therefore not what the learned estimator lives on **(inference)**.
* **OOD (trained or tuned at x0.5 and x2, evaluated at x1)**: L-est-OOD 65.1 % vs L-est-full 66.5 % (-1.4 pp [-4.9, 2.1]); T-adapt3-OOD 59.2 % vs T-adapt3-full 65.6 % (-6.4 pp [-12.2, -0.6]). L-est-OOD minus T-adapt3-OOD = +5.9 pp [1.1, 10.8]. The learned estimator transfers across dose with almost no loss; the classical tuning loses ~6 pp, but T-adapt3-OOD got a smaller tuning budget and a different probe (Section 7), so this contrast is confounded.
* **Dose curve (holdout 1100-1299, n=200 per level, 3 L-est training seeds)**: success % best-classical / L-est-full / L-est-eff / T-oracle: x0.5 71.0 / 64.3 / 67.5 / 92.0; x1 69.5 / 61.2 / 61.2 / 89.5; x2 53.0 / 60.3 / 56.8 / 91.0. Best-classical degrades strongly at x2; L-est is flatter. Paired L-est-full minus best-classical: -6.7 [-13.5, 0.2], -8.3 [-16.2, -0.3], +7.3 [-0.0, 14.7] pp. By the rule fixed in v7.1 a learned advantage appearing at only one dose (here x2, CI touching 0) is a single-point observation, not a general conclusion; at x1 and x0.5 L-est does not beat the classical method.
* **Single factors (x1, holdout n=200)**: L-est-full minus best-classical: a (encoder noise) -7.3 [-14.3, -0.3], b (per-link mass + friction) +5.5 [-1.2, 12.3], c (actuator noise) +1.0 [-6.3, 8.3] pp; L-est-eff -2.2 / +0.2 / +0.5. No single factor gives a learned win with CI above 0. T-oracle is 86-93 % in every cell, so headroom exists everywhere.
* **Ceiling diagnostic** (Section 9): a perfect parameter estimator inside the same probe-and-replay pipeline reaches only 74 % on CAL.

## 3. Step 0: the NaN / "unstable" warnings

* Two different sources. (i) Planning proxies: `SwingSim` rollouts and `_closed_loop` re-scoring in the whip planner simulate thousands of candidate swings, a fraction of which blow up numerically; the planner detects the clock jump and scores them `inf`/+10. These warnings are harmless to metrics (they are most of the 599k lines in `MUJOCO_LOG.TXT`, time stamps 0.005-0.02 s are candidate rollouts). (ii) **The true plant** (`arena/env.py` `step`) also blows up in some episodes: chain joint speeds reach ~500 rad/s when the whip cracks and RK4 at dt = 2.5 ms goes unstable. MuJoCo auto-resets the state and **arena does not flag this as diverged** (`diverged` stays 0), so the episode silently continues from a reset state.
* Measured with a counter on true-plant steps (clock jump back or non-finite state), CAL n=50: plant dt as in spike09/10a (SUBF=1): 4 % (T-nom, abc), 2 % (T-adapt, abc), 2 % (T-oracle, abc), 4 % (T-nom, no conditions). SUBF=2: 0/300 on CAL but 2/900 on the first holdout runs (nom 1/300, adapt 1/300; 0.22 %, below 0.5 % but with no margin). SUBF=4: 0 unstable episodes on CAL (300) and on all holdout / TEST-B / TEST / dose episodes.
* Fix (in `c10b.Real2.reset`, no change to arena): the true plant is integrated with dt/4 (substeps x4), control rate unchanged. All reported spike10b numbers after CAL tuning use SUBF=4. Unstable-episode fraction per method is in `table.md` (0.00 % for every method on TEST-B, TEST, holdout and every dose cell).
* Consequence: absolute numbers are not comparable to spike09b/10a (e.g. T-adapt with no conditions drops from 100 % to 94 % (SUBF=2) and 90 % (SUBF=4) on CAL because the nominal-model replay now differs slightly from the finer plant; this lowers the "perfect model" advantage of model-fit identification).

## 4. Classical side

* T-adapt3 = T-adapt2's probe/replay/plan pipeline with the model class extended to length scale, **per-link masses (10)**, viscous damping and **Coulomb chain friction** (`fit3`, 13 parameters, "full") or a **low-dimensional** log-linear mass gradient (5 parameters, "grad"). Bounded robust least squares over progressive prefixes of the probe; optional Gaussian priors (per-link spread, slope, friction centred at 2e-3 Nm).
* Identifiability on CAL (50 seeds, abc), tuned configs (`ident_cal_chosen.json`, `table.md`): "full": length scale 96 % of variance explained, log total mass ~100 %, mass slope 74 %, log damping 66 %, per-link mass deviation 47 %, **friction 0 % (not identifiable from the arm probe; estimate ~ prior)**. "grad": length 90 %, total mass 99.8 %, slope -15 % (not identifiable), damping 76 %, friction -1443 % (the estimate wanders far from truth; even with a prior). So: global mass, length and (partly) damping are identifiable; friction is not; per-link structure only partly, and only when the model is allowed to fit it ("full" beats "grad", also in success).
* Tuning budget (episodes on CAL): grad 900 (18 evaluations x 50, including 100 reference episodes of T-adapt/T-adapt2), full 750 (15 evaluations), total **1650 episodes** of the ~3000 allowed. Abandonment rule of v7.1 (neither parametrisation better than T-adapt2 on CAL) was **not triggered**: CAL success T-adapt2 60 %, grad 66 %, full 80 %. Note winner's curse: n=50 per evaluation, SE ~7 pp; holdout/TEST-B values are lower (full 69.7 % / 65.6 %, grad 52.7 % / 51.0 %).
* Holdout (n=300, 1100-1399): T-nom 55.7, T-robust 52.0, T-adapt 57.3, T-adapt2 57.7, T-adapt3-grad 52.7, **T-adapt3-full 69.7**, T-oracle 90.0 %. best-classical = T-adapt3-full.
* Symmetric classical OOD control T-adapt3-OOD: same "full" parametrisation, tuned on CAL seeds alternating x0.5 / x2 with the **reduced** protocol (350 episodes; probe stage, linear-loss test, friction prior only), evaluated at x1. TEST-B 59.2 % vs 65.6 % for the in-distribution tuned T-adapt3-full.

## 5. Learned side

* **L-est-full**: MLP (tanh, 2 hidden layers) from the probe history features (measured minus nominal-model replay, stride 2, 360 features for T=0.6 s) to the full plant vector theta (length scale, 10 log per-link masses, log damping, friction), then the same pipeline as T-adapt3 (replay-reconstructed chain state, same planner on the estimated plant). 12000 probe-only training episodes on seeds 500000-511999 (independent draw of chain error and conditions per seed, rep 0); hyper-parameters (width, weight decay, epochs) chosen on CAL (50 seeds x 4 independent draws) by label MSE; 3 training seeds. Probe (T=0.6 s, amplitude 0.6) = the tuned probe of T-adapt3-full.
* **L-est-eff**: same, regresses only length, total mass, damping, friction (4 outputs; planner gets a uniform chain). No per-link labels.
* **L-est-OOD**: like full, trained on 12000 episodes at x0.5 and x2 (seeds 520000+), selected on CAL at x0.5/x2, evaluated at x1.
* **R-PPO-hist**: spike09b/10 recipe (log-std init -3.5, floor -5, lr 1e-4, 4 epochs, 256x256 tanh, residual cap 12 Nm, zero-initialised output layer, history lags of arm q, qd) on top of best-classical (T-adapt3-full) under condition abc; 300 training scenarios (seeds 500000-500299, rep 0), 3.0 M steps = 8204 training episodes; 7 equally spaced checkpoints evaluated on CAL; selected checkpoint must beat the zero-residual initialisation by >= 5 pp.
  * Result: initialisation 78 % on CAL, checkpoints 82 / 78 / 72 / 74 / 76 / 82 / 76 %; selected 82 % = +4 pp (< 5 pp) with residual norm 0.61 Nm: **NOT LEARNED**. Training success (last 200 episodes) stayed at 0.59-0.72 around the base rate 0.66 of the pool.
  * One documented diagnostic round (allowed once): 4x batch (64 envs x 512 steps, minibatch 4096), everything else equal: init 78 %, checkpoints 72 / 82 / 74 / 76 / 70 / 78 / 76 %, selected 82 % = +4 pp: **NOT LEARNED** (residual 0.23 Nm). The main recipe was kept as R-PPO-hist; the diagnostic is not evaluated on TEST. No further rounds.
  * Only **training seed 0** was run (the first cut allowed by the wall-clock rule); the verdict for H12 rests on one seed and a checkpoint that is, by the card's own rule, "not learned". At TEST-B its success equals best-classical's (65.6 % vs 65.6 %).

## 7. Priors and information each side received (disclosure)

* **L-est-full is trained and evaluated on the same distribution** (same generator of chain errors and realistic conditions, disjoint seeds) and uses **simulator labels** (the full plant vector). Classical methods use no labels.
* **T-adapt3 gets more prior than it looks**: (i) a model class that contains the true plant structure exactly (uniform-length chain, per-link masses, viscous damping, Coulomb friction), (ii) box bounds equal to (a margin around) the support of the true error distribution (length +-20 %, log mass +-1.5, log damping +-2.2), (iii) a Gaussian friction prior centred at the distribution mean (2e-3 Nm), (iv) the measurement noise scales (sq, sv) tuned on CAL success, (v) the probe (duration, amplitude) tuned on CAL success. L-est receives the same probe and learns the distribution from 12000 labelled draws.
* **Both** use the same replay-based reconstruction of the chain state after the probe, and the same planner (CMA-ES + closed-loop re-scoring). The probe costs 0.6 s of the 2.5 s episode for both; **T-oracle has no probe and starts from the exact rest state**, so the oracle gap (+23.6 pp) is partly a probe cost no identification method can recover.
* The OOD pair is not perfectly symmetric: T-adapt3-OOD used 350 tuning episodes (reduced protocol) and its own probe (T=0.2 s), L-est-OOD kept the in-distribution probe (T=0.6 s) and the same hyper-parameter sweep as L-est-full.
* R-PPO-hist and L-est-eff use no per-link labels in the policy / planner input; R-PPO's reward uses the privileged tip distance.

## 8. Deviations from v6 / v7 / v7.1 (all of them)

1. **Plant integration step /4** (Section 3), introduced after the first holdout run showed 2/900 plant blow-ups at /2; that holdout run was discarded and redone. CAL tuning of T-adapt3-grad, T-adapt3-full, the reference evaluations of T-adapt/T-adapt2, and the identifiability numbers (default config) used the /2 plant; the OOD tuning, the holdout, L-est/PPO training and all locked runs used /4. The tuned configs were not re-tuned.
2. **Holdout n=300** (1100-1399) instead of 500 (v7 requires n >= 300 for the selection, but also asks for the same table on n=500). The "same table on holdout" is available for the classical methods and T-oracle (`holdout1.json`) and for L-est (`holdout_extra.json`, n=300); R-PPO-hist and T-adapt3-OOD were not evaluated on holdout.
3. **Order**: the dose curve was run after the locked TEST-B/TEST runs (it only uses holdout seeds and changes nothing frozen); v7.1 lists it before the freeze. Dose n=200 per level (lowest allowed by the user rule is 150, v7.1 needs >= 200), methods: best-classical (= T-adapt3-full), T-oracle, L-est-full, L-est-eff (3 training seeds each). Not included in the dose curve: T-adapt3-grad, the OOD variants, R-PPO-hist.
4. **R-PPO-hist: 1 training seed (not 3)** and **300 training scenarios** (spike09b used 1200) to fit the wall-clock cap; first cut of the user's order. The diagnostic round used one seed.
5. T-adapt3 tuning: "grad" with the full 6-stage protocol (900 episodes), "full" without the final optimiser-effort and probe-refinement stages (750 episodes); the greedy search for "full" spent one evaluation (`B soft_l1 fs=3`) on a configuration identical to the previous best (linear loss ignores f_scale).
6. L-est hyper-parameters were selected by CAL label MSE (z-space), not by CAL downstream success (50 episodes would be too noisy for 8 configurations x 3 variants); the CAL draw set has 200 draws (50 seeds x 4 independent plant draws), seeds 1000-1049 only.
7. L-est's chain-state reconstruction is by replay on the estimated plant (as T-adapt), i.e. L-est regresses plant parameters only, as specified. A "regress the state too" variant was not run.
8. T-oracle (full) has no probe (as in spike10a).
9. The eval script runs methods in priority order (best-classical, L-est-full, R-PPO first); this has no effect on results.
10. The earlier `step0_nan.json` is from a first diagnostic pass (SUBF=2 default at that time, counted by call site); `step0_nan_subf{1,2,4}.json` are the final measurements.

## 9. Limitations

* One realistic-condition family, one chain length/mass distribution, simulation only; friction magnitude (2e-3 Nm) is an estimate.
* TEST-B CI half-widths are ~4-5 pp; the +10 pp rule has power only against large effects. H11 and H12 are "not supported", not "refuted for all designs": the CI of L-est-full minus best-classical, [-3.5, 5.4] pp, excludes +10 pp, so a +10 pp effect is excluded for this L-est design.
* best-classical was chosen on holdout and is optimistic there (69.7 % vs 65.6 % on TEST-B).
* The ceiling diagnostic (CAL, n=50, `ceiling_cal.json`): the pipeline probe -> regress -> replay -> plan fed with the **true** theta reaches only 74 % [62, 86], about the level of T-adapt3-full (78 % [66, 88] on the same CAL seeds; L-est-full seed 0: 56 % [42, 70]), while T-oracle reaches 90 % [82, 98]. **(inference)** the gap between best-classical and T-oracle is largely caused by the probe time and the unobserved chain state at swing start, not by parameter error, so a better parameter estimator (learned or fitted) cannot close it; closing it would need a different design (shorter or no probe, state estimation, or residual policy that can use the information) and was not tested.
* R-PPO-hist: one seed, not learned by the card's rule in two recipes. This does not show that no residual policy could help.
* Machine was shared and heavily loaded; wall-clock numbers are inflated and noisy.
* The dose curve uses n=200 and 3 training seeds for L-est; single-factor cells use the same n.
