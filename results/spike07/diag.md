# spike07 diagnosis of the late-training PPO collapse (CAL + training seeds only; TEST untouched)

Written BEFORE the diagnostic runs (2026-10-30).

**Evidence from spike06** (`results/spike06/sweep2_*.log`, 1 seed per config): at later checkpoints CAL success falls to 0-30% for most configs while the earliest checkpoints (0.5-1.5M steps) are near the initialisation (~76%); the logged residual stays tiny (<= 0.9 Nm), so the collapse is a drift of the actor mean, not large exploration noise.

**Falsifiable hypothesis (primary):** the collapse is caused by *noisy policy-gradient drift under Adam* (small std -> the PPO ratio is hypersensitive to tiny mean changes, advantage signal across 32 very different plants is noise, nothing limits how far the mean moves per iteration), NOT by the cap-as-terminal bias. Predictions:
 (P1) run D4 (target-KL early stop) and/or D3 (4x batch) will show no collapse (all CAL checkpoints >= 50%, final >= init - 1 episode);
 (P2) run D2 (cap bootstrap only) will still collapse like the baseline D0;
 (P3) D5 (BC toward zero residual) will suppress the collapse but also the learning (residual norm ~0).
Falsified if D2 alone already fixes it (then the terminal-at-cap bias is the cause), or if no single change/combination fixes it (then the cause is something else and I report that).

Runs (hist variant, training seed 100, 2.5M steps, 4 equally spaced CAL checkpoints, lr 1e-4, log-std init -3.5; classical = best-classical of spike07): D0 baseline (= spike06 recipe), D2 cap bootstrap, D3 batch x4 (128 envs, minibatch 8192, same epochs), D4 target-KL 0.01 (stop epochs when approx-KL > 1.5*0.01), D5 BC coefficient 10 on mean(mu^2), D6 = D2+D3+D4.
Pass criterion (v5 spec, operationalised before seeing results): on CAL the final iterate is not worse than the initialisation (success >= init - 2 pp = one CAL episode) AND no CAL checkpoint and no point of the rolling training-success curve falls below 50%. Additionally (my addition, so that "fixed" does not mean "does nothing"): residual L2 norm on CAL must be > 0.5 Nm mean.

## Findings
1. Spike06's collapse reproduces (D0: 70/66/54/64% against init 76%; with larger lr/other seeds it goes to 0-30%). The residual L2 norm grows monotonically in every run (0.15 -> 0.35..1.1 Nm) while CAL success falls: the actor mean *drifts* away from the teacher and the drift hurts.
2. Primary hypothesis (noisy-gradient drift) is only partly supported. Supported: the 4x batch (D3, D9, E5) is the only single change that keeps CAL at 74% for the first 3 of 4 checkpoints (D9 74/74/74/66, replicate E5 on another seed 74/74/74/58) and slows the residual growth (0.15 -> 0.30 Nm). Not supported: target-KL early stop makes it worse (D4 66/52/6/8, D8), BC toward zero residual (D5, tiny help at best), more envs per batch (E1, 256 envs) is not better than 128, lower lr with 4x batch (E2) is not better.
3. Cap-as-terminal (P2 predicted: no effect) was *not* the cause: bootstrapping at the cap (D2) did not fix anything and was worse at the first checkpoint (34%; I did not find out why, inference: bootstrapping with the pre-step value amplifies critic error). Hypothesis P2 survives, "cap bootstrap" is dropped.
4. Reward arithmetic (my addition, not in the card): with spike06's reward +1/hit - 3|apex err| - 3 fail, the retuned T-robust has mean apex error 0.38 m, i.e. 1 - 3*0.38 = -0.15 reward per hit; with gamma .995 (horizon ~4 hits) the reward is not aligned with "reach 300 hits". Lowering the apex weight to 0.3 helped only together with the bigger batch (D7 vs D0 no help at batch 1x; D9 vs D3 better but a single-seed difference, E4 (apex 3, batch 4x, seed 101) went 74/72/62/24). gamma .999 + fail -20 (F1/F2) gave 74/66/68/66 and 76/74/72/64: no better than D9/E5.
5. More exploration noise (G1/G2: log-std0 -2.5, ~1 Nm) is worse than -3.5 (G1 46/54/12; G2 72/60/64).
6. **Pass criterion NOT met.** No configuration kept the *final* iterate >= init - 2 pp on CAL (best: D9/E5 final 66% / 58% vs 76%; F2 final 64%), and no CAL curve stayed >= 50% in all my 4 checkpoints for the best configs only because they stop at 2.5M steps. What I could establish is a configuration (D9/E5) where the first three quarters of training stay at the init level, and a mechanism consistent with the data: PPO's gradient signal for a <= 1 Nm residual on a 76%-successful chaotic juggling task is below the noise of the 32-128 plant batches (inference), Adam converts that noise into a random-walk-like drift of the actor mean (residual norm growth ~ sqrt(t)), and any drift lowers success because the teacher is near a local optimum of the CAL objective (it was CMA-tuned on exactly these CAL episodes). Larger batches reduce but do not remove the drift.
7. **Deviation (stated before the final runs):** the final R-PPO-fixed and R-oracle use the best available configuration (D9/E5: 128 envs x 256, minibatch 8192, lr 1e-4, log-std0 -3.5, apex weight 0.3, gamma .995, fail -3, 2.5M steps, 7 equally spaced CAL checkpoints with CAL selection) although the pass criterion was not reached. Per the card, TEST would not be run until fixed; I judged that "fixed" cannot be reached inside the compute budget and treat the unreachable criterion as a documented negative finding about PPO on this task rather than stopping the whole experiment (the commander's instruction allows a stated deviation). CAL checkpoint selection (7 equally spaced) is what guards against the late decline.

## SAC pilots (CAL only; separate from the PPO diagnosis but run in the same rounds)
S1 (auto-alpha, bc 0, lr 3e-5): CAL 0% at the first checkpoint (0.5M steps), residual 9.6 Nm. S2 (bc 1): 26%, 0.7 Nm. S3/S4 (noisier policy): auto-alpha diverged (alpha 337) and I stopped them before any checkpoint. S5 (fixed alpha 0.001, lr 1e-5, bc 1): 2% at 0.4M steps, 0.64 Nm. A first final attempt (aborted/, killed by me at the first checkpoint) showed CAL 0-4% already at 171k steps with residual 0.8-1.1 Nm, i.e. before the actor had been trained much: **the gain-0.01 output init gives ~1 Nm of residual from step 0 and this alone destroys the (chaotic, CMA-tuned) teacher**. All pilots S1-S5 are therefore confounded by that init and cannot be read as "SAC cannot learn". The mean head was then zero-initialised and the final R-SAC was a CAL-only sweep of 2 configs x 3 seeds (A: lr 3e-5, BC 1; B: lr 1e-4, BC 3); results in README/table.md. (Same init applies to PPO's actor, gain 0.01: the PPO residual at the first checkpoint is already 0.14-0.2 Nm; I did not re-run PPO with a zero init.)
Machine note: two final PPO/oracle attempts with 8 and 6 parallel processes were killed by the 10 GB memory cap (about 1.5 h lost); each process grows to ~1.5 GB late in training; the final runs used 3 processes per lock hold.
