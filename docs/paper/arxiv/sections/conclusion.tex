\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In a simulated arm benchmark with extended classical tooling, disclosed budgets and pre-specified rules, no control-level learned method beat the strongest classical baseline by +10\pp. On realistic whip, the largest rung of the headroom ladder is parameter estimation (+17.6\pp), and a decision rule that scores candidates across several plausible plants recovers most of it for either side, with the learned side ahead by +5.6\pp\ [0.6, 10.6] and a 10\pp\ advantage not excluded. The evidence is one simulator and mostly one task. The next experiments we consider most informative are a control-level learner that demonstrably closes part of the oracle gap, a second task with headroom in a second simulator, and equal-effort classical tuning for the estimator duel.
