\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}


\textbf{Arm and simulator.} All experiments run in MuJoCo with planar serial arms. The default arm has two links (0.5 and 0.4\,m, 1.0 and 0.8\,kg); juggling adds a racket as a third, wrist joint; the whip uses a three-link arm (0.40, 0.35 and 0.25\,m; 1.0, 0.8 and 0.5\,kg; 0.2\,kg tip payload). A task, a robot, a controller and a list of perturbations plug into one environment interface. Controllers see only the nominal model; perturbations change the true plant (the list of perturbations is in Appendix~\ref{app:replication}). Control runs at 5\,ms.

\textbf{Seven tasks and where each is used.} The task set is figure tracking (a tool-tip glyph), pole balancing, Push-T, ball juggling, ball catching, ball tossing into a basket, and whip (Fig.~\ref{fig:tasks}). Learned-versus-classical comparisons with a full protocol cover tracking, juggling and whip (settings S1--S8, Table~\ref{tab:cross}). Pole balancing was run only on the classical ladder. Catching, tossing and Push-T have classical pilots (5--20 demo seeds) and, in Appendix~\ref{app:breadth}, residual-PPO mini-comparisons that add breadth but not evidence for K2.

\textbf{Whip.} The arm flings a chain of ten capsules (0.10\,m, 0.015\,kg each, hinged at the tip). The \emph{chain tip} must pass within 0.08\,m of a target 1.15--1.70\,m from the shoulder, outside the arm's 1.0\,m reach, within 2.5\,s. The chain state is not observed. ``Realistic conditions'' add encoder noise (1e-3\,rad, 1e-2\,rad/s), actuator noise $U(0.01, 0.02)$\,Nm, Coulomb friction $2\text{e-}3\,\text{Nm} \times U(0.5, 1.5)$ and per-link mass factors $\exp U(-0.3, 0.3)$. These magnitudes are our choice, set before the results, and are not derived from hardware. Success is the primary metric.

\textbf{Classical ladder.} For tracking the ladder runs proportional-integral-derivative control (PID), computed torque (CTC) with a linear-quadratic regulator (LQR), written CTC+LQR, iterative LQR (iLQR), iLQR in a model-predictive-control loop (iLQR-MPC) and model-predictive path-integral control (MPPI). For tuning, C0 is the untuned teacher, C1 retunes the proportional and derivative gains Kp/Kd, or the LQR cost weights Q/R, with CMA-ES on the calibration seeds CAL, and C2 adds an integral term. For juggling and whip we use T-nom (nominal), T-robust (CMA-ES over the perturbation distribution) and T-adapt (online system identification from a short probe). For realistic whip we add T-adapt3 with an extended model class (length scale, ten chain-link masses, damping, Coulomb friction; 13 parameters). The suffix ``-full'' marks this complete 13-parameter model class, and its fitted-parameter pipeline T-adapt3-full is the baseline called R0 below. On whip the plan comes from CMA-ES keyframes (a handful of arm poses between which the swing is interpolated) tracked by computed torque.

\textbf{Learned methods.} Imitation students are a multilayer perceptron (MLP) trained by behaviour cloning (BC), MIP (two-step regression), flow matching with 1 or 10 steps, and a denoising-diffusion-implicit-model diffusion policy with ten steps (DDIM-10). Where stated, BC demonstrations are recorded with injected noise (DART) or relabelled iteratively (DAgger). Residual RL is PPO (SAC in sensitivity runs) on top of the strongest classical controller, with a residual capped at 12\,Nm in juggling and whip; the network and training recipe are in Appendix~\ref{app:budget}. Learned estimators map a short probe to the plant parameters. L-est is an MLP on the probe's tracking residuals (12{,}000 probe-only training episodes, simulator labels; in the tables L-est-full, trained with per-link chain-mass labels). L-dec4 is a gated recurrent unit (GRU) on the raw probe time series, trained with four times the data and three training seeds. Its decision-weighted loss weights each parameter error by how much it shifts the planned swing, and that weighting was computed from 1{,}600 oracle plans made with the true simulated parameters of training episodes, so L-dec4 uses true-plant information beyond the simulator parameter labels. Its 13{,}120 episode-equivalents (a probe-only episode counts as 0.24 of a full episode) are $48{,}000\times0.24+1{,}600$. Ensembles average the estimated parameters of the three frozen L-dec4 networks. B is this learned ensemble used with robust plan selection (Section~\ref{sec:k3}), and L-ens is the same ensemble without it. C-ens is the classical counterpart of B: robust plan selection over parameter samples drawn from a Laplace (Gaussian) approximation around the classical fit.

\textbf{Exploration scale.} The one residual-PPO parameter we found to matter most is the exploration log-std (Section~\ref{sec:protocol}); the rest of the recipe is in Appendix~\ref{app:budget}.

\textbf{Reproducibility.} Each result table is regenerated by a script from stored per-episode data, with the exception of runs that depend on untracked intermediate files; locked-set runs (TEST-B, TEST-C, TEST-D) were once-only and must not be repeated. All training seeds are at least 500{,}000 so that no training episode overlaps an evaluation set.

\textbf{Headroom ladder.} On whip we measure what each source of error costs with a fixed-order ladder on the same pipeline. R0 is the fitted-parameter pipeline. R1 gives it the true plant parameters, R2 adds the true chain state, R4 is an oracle planner with no probe time, and R5 is the oracle with the canonical (single, consistent) plan of Section~\ref{sec:k4}. Paired differences between rungs give the size of each block. We also run the reverse order (true chain state first). R3, an oracle that starts from rest and waits 0.6\,s, was run on HOLD and NEW only (Appendix~\ref{app:replication}), not on the locked sets.

\textbf{Statistics.} Paired percentile bootstrap, 95\% CI, per comparison, with $10^4$ to $2{\times}10^4$ resamples depending on the script. ``Recovered fraction'' is $(\text{X}-\text{R0})/(\text{R1}-\text{R0})$ with the same resample in numerator and denominator. Holm correction applies within a hypothesis family: three hypotheses for the TEST-C card and four for the TEST-D card, both on one-sided bootstrap $p$-values at family $\alpha=0.05$.
