Controller:

Per-joint Kp = w_n² M_ii, Kd = 2 w_n M_ii (critically damped on the nominal inertia). No inverse dynamics.

Computed torque: tau = ID(q, qd, qdd_ref + Kp e + Kd ed) with the nominal model; [Kp, Kd] is the LQR gain of the per-joint double integrator.

Task and weaknesses

The controllers always assume the nominal 1.5 kg payload; "true payload" changes the plant only. Delay is an actuator latency of whole 5 ms control periods.
